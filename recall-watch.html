Slip 02 · Honest limits

What this is not

Read this before you send anything. It applies to every scan.

a. Barcodes
~3% coverage
Exact matches are rare

Official records carry barcodes for only about 3% of recalls. Most findings are brand/title candidates marked REVIEW — you verify them, we do not auto-alarm.
b. No match
≠ safe
Nothing found means nothing found

A clean scan means no match in these records, not that a product is safe. Coverage is federal CPSC + FDA only.
c. Signal
not counsel
Risk intelligence, not legal advice

Reports flag potentially relevant recalls earlier. They are not compliance rulings and do not prevent suspensions.
